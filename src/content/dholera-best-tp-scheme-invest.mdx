If you are buying land in Dholera SIR, the single most important question is not *whether* to buy — it is
**which Town Planning (TP) scheme to buy in**. The scheme number decides whether your plot has working
utilities, what you may legally build, and crucially how easily you can resell. Two plots of identical size
a few kilometres apart can differ in value by a multiple, purely because of the scheme line on the
[cadastral map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-tp-map).

This is the buyer's comparison. For the statutory background on how reconstitution works, read our
[TP 1 to TP 6 explainer](https://dholeramap.com/blog/dholera-tp-schemes-explained) first; here we focus on
which scheme actually deserves your money in 2026.

## The 30-second answer

| | TP 1 | TP 2 | TP 6 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Best for** | End use, near-term building | Industrial & fab-supply investment | Long-horizon industrial land banking |
| **Utilities** | In the ground today | Trunk infrastructure underway | Not yet |
| **Liquidity** | Highest (resale market is live) | Rising fast with the fab | Lowest |
| **Entry price** | Premium | Mid | Lowest per square yard |
| **Key villages** | Ambli, Bhadiyad, Gogla, Kadipur | Hebatpur, Bhimnath | Bhangadh, Zankhi, Bavaliyari |

For most buyers the honest ranking is **TP 1 first, TP 2 second, TP 6 a patient third**. TP 3, TP 4 and TP 5
serve narrower theses we cover at the end.

## TP 1 — the only scheme where you can build now

TP 1 covers **Ambli, Bhadiyad, Bhimtalav, Gogla, Kadipur, Khun and Umargadh**, and it holds the 22.5 sq km
**Activation Area** — the one part of the entire 920 sq km SIR where trunk infrastructure is complete rather
than promised. We detail what is actually built there in
[Activation Area utilities](https://dholeramap.com/blog/dholera-activation-area-utilities): underground power,
treated and recycled water, SCADA monitoring and optic fibre to the plot boundary.

That matters for a practical reason. A non-agricultural (NA) permission and a building permission are far
easier to obtain when the plot already has legal road frontage and services. TP 1 plots carry 18m / 30m /
55m / 70m roads and predominantly Residential R-1 and commercial zoning, so under
[DGDCR 2024](https://dholeramap.com/blog/dholera-dgdcr-far-building-rules) an 18m-frontage plot gives an
indicative FAR of 1.20–1.80 and a 30m-frontage plot 2.00–2.50.

The cost of that certainty is price. TP 1 serviced plots trade at a premium over every other scheme — see
the current rate card at [Dholera plot prices 2026](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-plot-price).

**Buy TP 1 if** you intend to build within a few years, or you want the safest store of value with the most
active resale market.

## TP 2 — the fab supply-chain trade

TP 2 covers **Hebatpur, Bhimnath and Gorasu**, and it is where the largest single private investment in
Indian manufacturing history sits: the Tata-PSMC semiconductor fab, which we track daily in
[Tata Semiconductor fab status](https://dholeramap.com/blog/tata-semiconductor-dholera-fab-status). A fab
does not build alone — it pulls in an ecosystem of OSAT units, gases, chemicals, precision logistics and
employee housing, all of which need land immediately around it.

Hebatpur carries the widest road hierarchy in the SIR, from 18m local streets up to the **250m expressway
corridor**, and its published cadastre is the largest of any village — browse it on the
[Hebatpur village directory](https://dholeramap.com/village/hebatpur). Wide-frontage plots here attract the
highest allowable FAR, which is what an industrial end-user actually pays for.

The risk is timing. Fab supply chains move in years, not months, so TP 2 is a **liquidity trade**: you are
buying ahead of confirmed end-user demand. Treat it as a 3–7 year hold.

**Buy TP 2 if** you understand industrial land cycles, want fab-supply-chain upside, and can hold through a
slow lease-up. Its proximity to the
[expressway and airport corridor](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-expressway-airport-map) is the price floor.

## TP 6 — the cheapest land, with the longest wait

TP 6 covers **Bhangadh, Zankhi and southern Bavaliyari** — coastal heavy engineering and maritime supply
chain, with direct access to the Gulf of Khambhat. It is the largest scheme by area and carries the lowest
entry price per square yard of the three.

That low price is not a hidden bargain; it is the market pricing in the absence of infrastructure. TP 6 has
no completed trunk utilities and no anchor tenant on the scale of the fab. If you buy here, you are making a
pure land-banking bet on coastal industrialisation — a real thesis, but one with a payoff horizon that can
exceed a decade. The published parcels on the
[Zankhi village directory](https://dholeramap.com/village/zankhi) show what is actually available.

**Buy TP 6 if** you are a fund or a patient investor sizing a position in coastal industrial land at the
lowest possible basis, and you do not need near-term liquidity.

## What about TP 3, TP 4 and TP 5?

- **TP 3** (Dholera town, Sandhida, Sangasar) is the commercial and financial core. It offers the highest
  allowable FAR on the widest roads — but only valuable to a commercial end-user, not a residential buyer.
- **TP 4** (Mundi) is the renewable belt behind the 5,000 MW solar and green-hydrogen ambition. It is the
  most price-volatile scheme; bid only if you follow renewable policy closely.
- **TP 5** (Bavaliyari) is the aerotropolis play — aviation MRO, air cargo and warehousing around the
  greenfield Dholera International Airport. Its thesis now rests on an operational airport, which is a
  genuinely stronger position than three years ago.

## The three checks that apply to every scheme

Whichever scheme you choose, the diligence sequence is identical, and skipping the first step is the most
common way buyers lose money in Dholera:

1. **Verify the 7/12 (Satbara) and 8-A records** on the Gujarat AnyRoR portal *before* paying any token.
   Reconstitution changes boundaries, so the old survey map is not proof of what the seller owns. Our
   [7/12 verification guide](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-7-12-anyror-land-records) walks the portal
   screen by screen.
2. **Confirm the Final Plot schedule**, not just the survey number. The gazetted Form 4 / Form 5 schedule —
   after the statutory ~50% land deduction for roads and civic infrastructure — is the document of title.
3. **Cross-check the price against the scheme rate card** in the
   [2026 plot price guide](https://dholeramap.com/blog/dholera-plot-price-september-2026) before you
   negotiate. A quote that is far below the scheme norm usually means a boundary, zoning or title problem.

For ground-level parcel scrutiny, the vetted consultants in our
[RERA broker directory](https://dholeramap.com/brokers) can inspect a plot on your behalf.

## How to check which scheme a plot is in

Every published parcel in the SIR is searchable by survey number or Final Plot on the
[interactive DholeraMap atlas](https://dholeramap.com). Open a parcel and the record states its scheme,
abutting road width and DGDCR envelope — the same figures the
[cadastral guide](https://dholeramap.com/guide) reproduces as statutory tables. If a seller's claim about a
plot's scheme does not match the map, trust the map.
