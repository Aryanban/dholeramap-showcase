The case for Dholera SIR is genuinely strong — which is exactly why it is also surrounded by
hype. This is the investment case as the data supports it: what is actually being built, what
is driving land value, and the risks that the brochures leave out.

## The one-paragraph thesis

Dholera SIR is India's first greenfield industrial smart city, built on **920 sq km** across
**22 revenue villages** under the **Gujarat Special Investment Region Act, 2009**. Land value
here is not driven by residential demand — it is driven by **delivered industrial
infrastructure**. Where trunk infrastructure is live, serviced industrial land already trades
*above* residential land elsewhere in the SIR. The catalysts are real, anchored by a
₹91,000 crore semiconductor fab. The honest caveat is timing: the region is **partially
operational**, so the entry price you pay today often prices in servicing that has not yet
arrived.

## The four real catalysts

**1. The Tata Electronics semiconductor fab (TP 2 Activation Area).**
A ~**₹91,000 crore** facility under construction in the TP 2 Activation Area — the single
largest private industrial investment anchoring the region. It is not just a factory; it is
an ecosystem that pulls ancillary vendors, logistics and housing demand into the surrounding
villages. See the [Tata semiconductor location map](https://dholeramap.com/tata-semiconductor-dholera-map).

**2. The Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway.**
**109 km**, 4-lane and access-controlled, cutting the Ahmedabad–Dholera journey to roughly
1.5 hours. Connectivity is what converts a remote greenfield region into a commuter and
freight corridor. See the [expressway and airport route map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-expressway-airport-map).

**3. The greenfield international airport at Navagam.**
Supporting the TP 5 aerotropolis — airport-support industry, MRO, cargo and logistics. An
airport is what turns an industrial estate into a city.

**4. Statutory permanence.**
This is the most underrated catalyst and the least marketed. Dholera is not a developer
project that can be abandoned — it is a **statutory region** under the SIR Act 2009, with
**DSIRDA** as the planning authority and **DICDL** executing trunk infrastructure. Six
gazetted Town Planning schemes (TP 1–TP 6) mean the road network, zoning and building
controls are fixed by law, not redrawn by a promoter's quarterly decision.

## What actually drives the land price

If you remember one thing, make it this:

> **Delivered infrastructure sets the price — not the zone label, and not the village name.**

The September 2026 asking rates make this concrete (indicative broker asks, per square yard):

| Parcel type | Asking ₹/sq yd |
| --- | --- |
| TP 1 Activation Area, serviced residential | 11,000 – 16,000 |
| TP 1 on 55 m TP road frontage | 14,000 – 16,000+ |
| Serviced industrial, live trunk corridor | up to ~21,000 |
| TP 3 – TP 5 broad band, residential | 9,000 – 12,000 |
| Peripheral TP 5 / TP 6 | 8,000 – 9,500 |
| Outside the SIR boundary, agricultural | 5,000 – 9,000 |

Read the **inversion**: serviced industrial land in the Activation Area trades *above*
typical residential land elsewhere. And note that building entitlements follow the road —
under **DGDCR**, your permissible **FAR is 1.20–4.00** and height **15–70 m**, set by the
**abutting road width**. Two same-sized plots in the same village can therefore have very
different development rights, and very different values.

For the full village-level picture, see the
[village-wise land price guide](https://dholeramap.com/blog/dholera-village-wise-land-prices).

## The mechanics that most investors skip

**Land is pooled, not acquired.** Your Original Plot is reconstituted under the Gujarat
town-planning and SIR statutes: roughly **half the gross area goes to roads, utilities and
parks**, and you receive back a smaller, serviced Final Plot.

**Halve the area, double the effective rate.** A parcel quoted at ₹3,000/sq yd that yields a
50% Final Plot is really **₹6,000/sq yd** of buildable land. This single mechanic is why
"Dholera land multiplied fifty times" headlines mislead — they compare the price of a *raw
agricultural bigha* against the price of a *pooled, NA-converted, serviced square yard*. Two
different assets. The worked numbers are in the
[plot price guide](https://dholeramap.com/blog/dholera-plot-price-september-2026).

**There is no audited price index.** Dholera has no verified transaction series. The portals
that publish appreciation figures measure different property mixes across localities largely
*outside* the SIR and disagree sharply with each other. Treat any single growth number as
marketing until you see what it actually measures.

## The risks the marketing leaves out

- **Only part of the SIR is serviced.** Trunk infrastructure is live in the TP 1/TP 2
  Activation Area; TP 3–TP 6 are gazetted but still being built out. Paying a serviced price
  for an unserviced parcel is the most common way to lose money here.
- **Asking prices can price in the future.** With no transaction index, some pockets already
  assume infrastructure that has not been delivered.
- **The contribution is permanent.** The roughly half of the Original Plot contributed to
  public infrastructure is not recovered in cash.
- **Transaction costs are real.** Budget stamp duty and registration, the 10%-of-jantri NA
  premium (from November 2024), and remember **jantri doubled statewide in April 2023** — an
  older valuation understates your cost.
- **Boundary marketing.** Land just outside the SIR line is sold as "in Dholera" but gets none
  of the infrastructure, NA ease or reconstitution.

## How to invest in Dholera properly

The full buyer's framework — with the specific checks and the order to run them — is
[how to evaluate plots for sale in Dholera](https://dholeramap.com/blog/plots-for-sale-in-dholera).
In summary: choose your **tier** (serviced TP 1/2, the industrial corridor, or long-hold
peripheral), then verify the **individual parcel** — its TP scheme and abutting road on the
[TP map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-tp-map) (which sets your DGDCR build rights), the
**7/12 pulled from AnyRoR yourself](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-7-12-anyror-land-records),
and the **gazetted Form 4/5 final plot schedule** — before the deed, not after.

If you are investing from abroad, also read the [NRI investment guide](https://dholeramap.com/blog/nri-investment-guide-dholera)
— FEMA draws a hard line between agricultural survey land and an NA-converted Final Plot, and
that distinction decides whether you may buy at all.

## The bottom line

Dholera SIR is one of the few Indian real-estate stories where the underlying catalysts — a
semiconductor fab, an expressway, an airport and statutory permanence — are genuinely
delivered or under construction rather than merely proposed. The investors who do well here
are the ones who **price the serviced Final Plot, verify the revenue record themselves, and
accept the hold period**. The ones who lose money are the ones who buy raw agricultural land
on a multiple quoted from a serviced plot.
