The Tata Semiconductor fab at Dholera is the single largest private investment in the
region's history, and the anchor of its transition from planned city to manufacturing
economy. Here is the verified status as of September 2026.

## The project at a glance

| Parameter | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Investor | Tata Semiconductor Manufacturing Pvt Ltd (Tata Electronics) |
| Technology partner | **PSMC** (Powerchip Semiconductor Manufacturing Corp., Taiwan) |
| Total investment | **₹91,000 crore** (~$11 billion) |
| Capacity | **50,000 wafers per month** (~300 crore chips/year) |
| Process nodes | Starting at **55nm and 90nm**, scaling to **28nm** |
| Wafer size | 300 mm |
| Location | Dholera SIR, **Town Planning Scheme 2A** |
| SEZ area | **66.166 hectares** notified |
| Construction status | **Underway** |
| Commercial operations | Targeted **mid-2028** |

## Investment and approval

The Union Cabinet approved the fab on **29 February 2024** as one of three semiconductor
units cleared that day, with a cumulative approval value of ₹1,26,000 crore. The Dholera
fab alone is **₹91,000 crore**.

The financing structure matters for understanding who actually carries the risk: the
Centre funds 50% and the state 20% under India's semiconductor incentive scheme. Tata's
own contribution of ₹35,400 crore covers **both** the Dholera fab (₹91,000 crore) and the
Assam OSAT unit (₹27,000 crore) together valued at ₹1,18,000 crore.

## What is actually being built

The fab will initially run **mature nodes — 55nm and 90nm — before scaling to 28nm**.
Tata's own annual report confirms the intent to "start our chip journey at the 28nm node,"
while PSMC states 28nm is the most advanced node it will offer here; reporting indicates
the launch mix will skew toward 90nm.

Two corroborating details:

- **PSMC is the technology partner**, supplying process technology across several nodes.
  Around **300 fresh Indian graduates are being trained at PSMC's Taiwan fab** — a
  tangible sign the project is past paperwork and into workforce build-out.
- **ASML signed an MoU in May 2026** to supply lithography tools for the 300 mm fab.

## Location within Dholera SIR

The site sits inside **Town Planning Scheme 2A**, consistent with the high-access
industrial corridor that anchors the scheme.

- The **Ministry of Commerce notified a 66.166-hectare SEZ** covering the fab plots on
  **9 April 2026**, with the SEZ letter of approval granted in March 2026. The
  notification also designates an **inland container depot** — a direct customs
  facilitation for import of equipment and export of chips.
- The Gujarat government has **leased 163.5 acres to Tata Electronics on a long-term
  basis**, valued at zero for the transaction and pledged to five foreign banks in
  February 2026 as project security.

An ecosystem is forming around it: a **363-acre vendor park** for roughly 450 suppliers is
under development.

## Construction status and timeline

**Construction is underway.** In a written reply to the Gujarat state legislature on
**21 March 2025**, the state government confirmed: *"The construction of a unit of M/s Tata
Semiconductor Manufacturing Pvt Ltd is underway and it is estimated to be completed in the
second quarter of 2027-28."*

The ground-breaking ceremony took place in **March 2024**. At **SEMICON India 2026 in
September 2026**, Tata Semiconductor CEO/MD Randhir Thakur confirmed the project is
"progressing as scheduled… on track to meet the scheduled commitments we have made to the
government."

> **Read the timeline carefully — there are three different milestones, often conflated:**
> - **Construction completion:** Q2 of FY2027-28 (Gujarat government, March 2025)
> - **Trial production:** H1 2027
> - **Commercial operations:** mid-2028 (Union Minister Ashwini Vaishnaw, July 2026)
>
> Claims of "50% civil construction complete" or "pilot production by late 2026" are **not
> confirmed in official disclosures.** The original internal target of end-2026 has
> visibly moved.
