Dholera attracts two kinds of money: patient capital that reads the statutes, and
speculative capital that reads the brochures. This is the former. Everything below comes
from the same cadastral database that powers [DholeraMap](https://dholeramap.com) — no
projections presented as facts.

## What is real in 2026

**Built and operational:**
- The **109 km Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway**, 4-lane and access-controlled.
- The **22.5 sq km Activation Area** in TP 1 / TP 2A: underground power, recycled water,
  optic fibre, effluent networks, SCADA monitoring.
- **Dholera International Airport** at Navagam, anchoring the TP 5 aerotropolis.
- **5000 MW-scale renewable capacity** in the TP 4 solar belt.

**Statutorily in place:**
- Six sanctioned preliminary **TP schemes** (TP 1–6) covering 22 revenue villages.
- **DGDCR** development controls tying FAR (1.20–4.00) and height (15–70 m) to road width.
- Gazette **Form 4 / Form 5** schedules for final plot reconstitution.

## The single most important pricing mechanic: OP-to-FP

Almost every first-time buyer overpays by ignoring this. When a TP scheme reconstitutes
land:

> Your **Original Plot (OP)** contributes roughly **50% of its gross area** to public
> infrastructure. What you receive is a smaller, serviced **Final Plot (FP)**.

The correct question is never "what is the per-square-yard rate here?" It is:

**What is the rate per square yard of the *final plot* I will actually receive?**

An OP quoted at ₹3,000/sq yd that yields a 50% FP is effectively ₹6,000/sq yd of
deliverable land — before you confirm the FP schedule exists. Always price the FP, and
verify the schedule on the [7/12 record](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-7-12-anyror-land-records).

## The five risks to underwrite

### 1. Reconstitution risk
Boundaries and final plot numbers change during TP implementation. A survey number you buy
today may map to a different FP than expected. **Mitigation:** verify Form 4/5 status
before the deed, not after.

### 2. Road-width risk
Your plot's entire building envelope — and therefore its resale value — depends on the
road it abuts. A 70m-road plot supports up to 4.00 FAR; an 18m-road plot far less.
**Mitigation:** check abutting road width on the
[TP map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-tp-map).

### 3. Zoning mismatch risk
TP 4 is renewable logistics, TP 5 is aviation support, TP 2 is heavy industry. Buying a
residential dream plot in a heavy-industrial corridor is a zoning problem that no amount
of money fixes. **Mitigation:** read the scheme's dominant zoning before you fall in love
with a price.

### 4. Liquidity risk
Dholera's secondary market is thin. Exit can take quarters, not weeks, and prices are
opaque. **Mitigation:** buy at a price where a 30% drawdown is survivable, and prefer
Activation Area plots with clear FP status.

### 5. Title risk
Speculative parcels trade on power of attorney with broken mutation chains. This is the
most common failure mode in the region. **Mitigation:** never buy without a clean,
directly-downloaded 7/12 and a complete title chain.

## How to price a plot in 2026

1. Establish the **TP scheme** and **abutting road width** — these set the legal building
   envelope.
2. Compute the **OP-to-FP deduction** and price the final plot area.
3. Confirm the **7/12 survey number matches the cadastral map** and the FP schedule.
4. Benchmark against the
   [2026 plot price rate card](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-plot-price), which prices by
   scheme, not by district average.

## The verdict

Dholera is a **genuine, statutorily-backed industrial city** — not a speculative mirage.
The expressway, the airport, and the Activation Area utilities are real. The returns exist,
but they accrue to buyers who underwrite the **final plot**, the **road width**, and the
**title chain** — the three things that actually determine what a Dholera plot is worth.

Do the verification work on the map first. It is free, and it eliminates most of the
deals that go wrong.
