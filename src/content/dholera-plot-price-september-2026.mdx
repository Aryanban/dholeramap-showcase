Dholera land does not have one price — it has two, and they answer different questions.
This is the 2026 breakdown: what the development authority officially charges,
what the market actually asks, and the statutory deduction that determines what you
finally receive.

## The official DICDL rate card (2026)

The only authoritative published price is the **Dholera Industrial City Development
Limited (DICDL)** allotment rate, published on the official Gujarat government portal and
last updated **24 August 2026**. These are allotment (asking) rates for a **99-year
leasehold**, quoted per square metre:

| Land use | ₹/sq m (official) | Approx ₹/sq yd |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CBD / City Centre | 8,000 | ~6,690 |
| Tourism & Hospitality | 8,000 | ~6,690 |
| Residential | 6,000 | ~5,016 |
| High Access Corridor (HAC) | 6,000 | ~5,016 |
| Industry / Physical Infrastructure | 4,000 | ~3,344 |
| Knowledge & IT | 4,000 | ~3,344 |
| Recreation & Sports | 4,000 | ~3,344 |
| Solar Energy Park | 400 | ~334 |

*(1 sq yd = 0.836 sq m.)*

Three things the official rate card does **not** tell you:

1. **It excludes GST, registration, stamp duty, and land-filling cost.**
2. **Land is allotted "as-is, where-is"** — an unserviced parcel and a serviced one carry
   the same official rate.
3. **There is no per-TP-scheme official rate.** DICDL prices by land-use zone only. Every
   "TP-wise" price table you see online is private broker data, not a government rate.

Annual lease rent is **₹1/sq m/year**, and commercial/residential allotment is by auction
or tender only, with the reserve price set case-by-case by the Land Management Committee —
so the published card is a floor, not a transaction price.

**Read the inversion carefully:** serviced industrial land in the Activation Area
(₹21,000/sq yd) trades *above* typical residential land elsewhere. Delivered
infrastructure — not the zone label — sets the price. Recreation-zoned land is cheap for a
reason: permitted uses are restricted.

## The OP-to-FP deduction: the number that decides your real cost

Under the **GTPUD Act 1976** and the **SIR Act 2009**, Dholera uses **land pooling**, not
cash acquisition. The mechanic:

> Roughly **50% of your Original Plot (OP)** is deducted for public infrastructure — roads,
> utilities, parks. You receive back a smaller, serviced **Final Plot (FP)**. There is no
> cash compensation for the deducted half; a betterment charge is levied on top.

This is why the headline "Dholera land has multiplied 8×/50×" claims are misleading — they
splice two different assets: **raw agricultural land per bigha** against a **pooled,
NA-converted, serviced final plot per square yard**. The deduction, the development cost,
and the margin *are* the growth.

> **Price the Final Plot, not the Original Plot.** An OP quoted at ₹3,000/sq yd that yields
> a 50% FP is effectively ₹6,000/sq yd of deliverable land — before you confirm the FP
> schedule even exists.

## Recent market movement

Independent portals show meaningful appreciation, but treat both with caution — they track
**listing asks across a locality that is largely outside the SIR**, and the two disagree
sharply because they measure different property mixes:

- **99acres** (plots/land only): **+5.9% over 1 year**, +63.6% over 3 years; average asking
  rate ~₹8,100/sq yd.
- **Housing.com** (includes villas/apartments): **+29.98% over 1 year**, average ~₹1,098/sq ft.

No official audited price index exists for Dholera SIR land. Between jantri (a valuation
floor, not a market price), portal asks, and developer marketing, **no source publishes a
verified transaction price for a named Dholera parcel.**

## Statutory charges on top of the price

- **Stamp duty:** 4.9% on the higher of jantri or agreement value (+1% registration; the
  1% is waived for a female sole owner).
- **NA premium:** reduced from 30% to **10% of jantri**, effective November 2024.
- Statewide **jantri rates doubled in April 2023** — a material cost jump if your deed
  references an old valuation. Verify current figures on the GARVI portal.

## How to actually verify a plot before you pay

1. Confirm the **TP scheme** and **abutting road width** on the
   [DholeraMap TP map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-tp-map) — these set your legal
   building envelope under DGDCR (FAR 1.20–4.00, height 15–70 m).
2. Pull the **7/12 directly from AnyRoR** — never accept a photocopy. Follow the full
   [7/12 verification workflow](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-7-12-anyror-land-records).
3. Confirm the **Form 4/5 final plot schedule** — whether reconstitution is gazetted, and
   what the FP number and area actually are.
4. Cross-check the survey number against the
   [interactive cadastral atlas](https://dholeramap.com) before the deed, not after.
