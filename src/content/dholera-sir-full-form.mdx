## Dholera SIR full form

**Dholera SIR** stands for **Dholera Special Investment Region**. It is India's first
greenfield industrial smart city, spread across roughly **920 square kilometres** in
Ahmedabad district, Gujarat. If you have heard people refer to it simply as "Dholera
SIR" or search it as **dholera sir**, this is the meaning: a region given special
statutory investment status rather than an ordinary town.

> **Quick answer:** Dholera SIR = **Dholera Special Investment Region**. "SIR" is a
> statutory planning designation under the **Gujarat Special Investment Region Act, 2009**,
> not a marketing label. The region covers ~920 sq km across 22 revenue villages in
> Ahmedabad district and is planned through six Town Planning schemes (TP 1 to TP 6).

The "SIR" is the important part. A Special Investment Region is not a marketing label —
it is a statutory planning designation created by the **Gujarat Special Investment Region
Act, 2009**. Land inside an SIR is planned, zoned, and regulated by a dedicated
development authority rather than by ordinary municipal or panchayat rules.

> covers the full-form answer, the SIR vs SEZ distinction, all six TP schemes and every
> one of the 22 villages in one place. This post goes deeper on the legal background.

## Where is Dholera SIR on the map?

Dholera SIR sits in the south-west of Ahmedabad district, Gujarat, along the Gulf of
Khambhat coast, roughly **100 km south-west of Ahmedabad city** and about **130 km from
Gandhinagar**. The interactive [Dholera SIR map](https://dholeramap.com) plots the full
920 sq km region across **22 revenue villages** and the six Town Planning schemes, so you
can see exactly where the Activation Area ends and ordinary agricultural land begins.

The region is a designated node on the **Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC)**, which
is why it appears in national infrastructure planning alongside the dedicated freight
corridor project.

## SIR vs SEZ vs smart city — what is the difference?

These three terms are constantly mixed up, and the difference directly affects what you
are allowed to do with a plot:

| Term | Full form | What it allows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **SIR** | Special Investment Region | A statutory *region* under the Gujarat SIR Act, 2009. Includes industrial, residential, commercial and civic zones, plus a development authority and master plan. |
| **SEZ** | Special Economic Zone | A *fenced enclave* with customs duty and tax benefits for export-oriented units only. Not a city — no residential or civic master planning. |
| **Smart city** | (mission label) | An *urban upgrade* concept under India's Smart Cities Mission. Dholera is called a "greenfield smart city" because it is built from scratch with integrated utilities, not retrofitted. |

A Dholera plot can sit inside the SIR **and** benefit from smart-city infrastructure, but
being in the SIR does not automatically make it an SEZ. If an agent promises "SEZ
benefits", ask which gazetted notification they are referring to.

## Who administers Dholera SIR?

Two bodies, often confused:

- **DSIRDA** — Dholera Special Investment Region Development Authority, the statutory
  planning authority that sanctions town planning schemes and building permissions.
- **DICDL** — Dholera Industrial City Development Limited, the project execution company
  that builds the trunk infrastructure (roads, utilities, SCADA).

Land-related permissions flow through DSIRDA; the physical trunk infrastructure is built
by DICDL. Village-level revenue records, however, still originate from the Gujarat revenue
department — which is why a [7/12 (Satbara) land
record](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-7-12-anyror-land-records) check remains the
statutory proof of ownership even inside the SIR.

## How Dholera SIR is structured: TP 1 to TP 6

The region is planned through **six Town Planning (TP) schemes**. If you are buying land,
the TP scheme your plot falls inside determines almost everything about it — road
frontage, permissible height, and zoning.

| Scheme | Focus | Typical road widths |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **TP 1** | Residential R-1, city centre, Activation Area | 18m, 30m, 55m, 70m |
| **TP 2A / 2B** | High-access corridor, semiconductor fab, heavy industry | 18m–250m (expressway) |
| **TP 3** | Commercial core, institutional finance | 18m, 30m, 55m |
| **TP 4** | Renewable energy, solar park logistics | 18m, 30m, 55m, 70m |
| **TP 5** | Aerotropolis — airport support, MRO, cargo | 18m, 30m, 55m |
| **TP 6** | Heavy engineering, coastal industrial | 18m, 30m, 55m |

You can inspect every sanctioned scheme boundary and search any survey number or Final
Plot on the [Dholera TP map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-tp-map).

## What "SIR" status means for you as a land buyer

**1. Statutory reconstitution of plots.** Under the Gujarat Town Planning and Urban
Development (GTPUD) Act 1976, agricultural survey parcels inside a TP scheme are
reconstituted. Roughly half the gross area is contributed to public infrastructure;
landowners receive a serviced **Final Plot (FP)** with legal road frontage and clear title.

**2. Faster non-agricultural (NA) permissions.** Because the region is pre-planned, NA
approval for a serviced final plot is far more straightforward than for ordinary
agricultural land outside an SIR.

**3. Building controls follow the road.** Under **DGDCR** (the development control
regulations), your permissible Floor Area Ratio (FAR/FSI) — between **1.20 and 4.00** — and
maximum building height (**15 m to 70+ m**) are determined by the width of the road your
plot abuts.

**4. Title due diligence is still on you.** SIR status does not override the revenue
records. Boundaries and Final Plot numbers legitimately change during reconstitution, so a
survey number that looks correct on an old map may already have been reconstituted into a
different Final Plot.

## The infrastructure that makes the SIR real

- **Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway** — 109 km, 4-lane (expanding to 8), access-controlled,
  cutting Ahmedabad–Dholera travel to roughly 1.5 hours. See the
  [expressway and airport route map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-expressway-airport-map).
- **Dholera International Airport** at Navagam, supporting the TP 5 aerotropolis.
- **Regional Rapid Transit System (RRTS)** — dedicated freight and passenger corridor.
- **Tata Electronics semiconductor fab** — a ~₹91,000 crore facility in the TP 2 Activation
  Area, the anchor industrial tenant. See the
  [Tata semiconductor location map](https://dholeramap.com/tata-semiconductor-dholera-map).
- **Activation Area utilities** — in TP 1 / TP 2A: underground power, recycled water,
  optical fibre, and effluent networks with SCADA monitoring.

## Is Dholera SIR complete? Status in 2026

Dholera SIR is **partially operational, not complete**. The honest position for a land
buyer in 2026:

- The **Activation Area** (largely TP 1 and TP 2A) has trunk infrastructure, powered
  industrial plots and working utilities.
- The **semiconductor fab** is under active construction, which has driven the sharpest
  land-price movement in the surrounding villages.
- The **expressway** is functional and the **airport** site at Navagam is under
  development.
- TP 3 through TP 6 are planned and gazetted in varying stages — trunk infrastructure here
  is still being rolled out, so prices reflect future potential more than present
  servicing.

This is exactly why verifying a plot's TP scheme and servicing status matters more than the
village name. For current land rates by scheme, see the

## "Dholera SIR", "Dholera city", and common spelling variations

Search data shows the same place is searched many ways — **dholera sir**, **dhulera**,
**dholers sir**, **dholera smart city**, **dholera city map** — and they all refer to the
Dholera Special Investment Region. There is only one statutory SIR; "Dholera smart city"
and "Dholera city" are informal names for the same planned region, and
[dholeramap.com](https://dholeramap.com) indexes all of it.

Two practical distinctions that trip buyers up:

- **Dholera SIR vs Dholera town** — the historic Dholera town predates the SIR; the SIR is
  the 920 sq km statutory region around it, not the old settlement.
- **Inside vs outside the SIR boundary** — land just outside the boundary does not get SIR
  infrastructure, NA ease or reconstitution benefits, even when it is marketed as "in
  Dholera". Check the plot against the map boundary before you pay.
