The land parcel of 1,426 hectares was handed over entirely by the State Government free of cost and encumbrances, eliminating the acquisition bottlenecks typical of greenfield civil aviation infrastructure.

## 2026 Operational Timeline & Commissioning Sequence

A civil aerodrome moves through strict regulatory gateways mandated by the Directorate General of Civil Aviation (DGCA) and the International Civil Aviation Organization (ICAO). 

The operational roadmap for Dholera International Airport spans three sequential phases:

### Phase 1: Physical Handover & Navigational Calibration (Q1–Q2 2026)
- **Civil Works Completion**: The 3,200-metre main runway, parallel taxiways, rapid exit taxiways, and the 12-aircraft parking apron have reached physical completion.
- **Air Traffic Management (ATM)**: AAI's dedicated Air Traffic Control (ATC) tower and technical block are equipped with Doppler Very High Frequency Omni Range (DVOR), Distance Measuring Equipment (DME), and Instrument Landing Systems (ILS Cat-I).
- **Flight Inspection Calibration**: AAI dedicated calibration aircraft conduct multiple validation sorties to certify ILS glide path and localizer accuracy.

### Phase 2: DGCA Aerodrome Licensing & Cargo Launch (Mid 2026)
- **Public Aerodrome License**: DGCA issues the commercial operating license following full-scale emergency response, fire category (Category 9), and perimeter security drills.
- **Cargo Operations First**: Prior to regular passenger operations, the airport begins operations as a dedicated multimodal air cargo terminal. This directly supports the logistical transit of specialized equipment and raw materials for the Dholera Activation Area and Tata Electronics semiconductor fab in TP 2.

### Phase 3: Commercial Passenger Flights & Regional Hub (Late 2026 onwards)
- **UDAN / Regional Feeder Connectivity**: Inaugural passenger links connecting Ahmedabad-Dholera with Mumbai, Delhi, Bengaluru, and Hyderabad.
- **Overspill Traffic Relief**: As Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International (SVPI) Airport in Ahmedabad approaches runway airside saturation, international and domestic cargo flights alongside long-haul charter flights are channeled to Dholera.

## Technical Specifications: The Code 4E Runway

Unlike smaller regional airstrips, Dholera Airport is engineered from Day 1 as an international wide-body facility:

1. **Wide-Body Aircraft Clearance**: The 3,200m × 45m runway is classified under **ICAO Code 4E**, capable of accommodating wide-body aircraft such as the **Boeing 777-300ER, Boeing 787 Dreamliner, Airbus A330, and Airbus A350**, alongside standard narrow-body aircraft (Airbus A320, Boeing 737 Max).
2. **Phase 2 Expansion (Code 4F)**: The gazetted master plan reserves space for a second parallel runway measuring **4,000 metres**, classified under Code 4F to support the Airbus A380 and Boeing 747-8F freighters.
3. **Dedicated Cargo Terminal Complex**: Approximately **75 hectares of land** within the airport boundary is reserved exclusively for logistics providers, cold-storage pharmaceutical facilities, bonded warehouses, and automated container handling.

## 4 Critical Due Diligence Rules for Airport-Zone Land Buyers

Brokers frequently market agricultural land in peripheral villages with the pitch: *"Opposite the Dholera Airport boundary!"*

Before transacting near the airport corridor, verify these four non-negotiable statutory requirements:

### 1. DGCA Funnel Zone Building Height Restrictions
Land directly beneath aircraft approach and takeoff paths falls under the Obstacle Limitation Surfaces (OLS) mandated by the Civil Aviation Authority and DGDCR 2024 Chapter 14:
- In the inner horizontal and conical surfaces, permissible building heights are severely restricted regardless of high nominal FAR.
- Never purchase a parcel expecting to build high-rise commercial structures without verifying the **Colour Coded Zoning Map (CCZM)** issued by AAI for Dholera Airport.

### 2. Expressway Frontage Is Not Road Access
Parcels physically abutting the National Expressway 8 right-of-way cannot create private driveway cuts into the expressway. Access is strictly permitted through gazetted 30m, 55m, or 70m internal Town Planning roads feeding into designated interchange service lanes.

### 3. The 50% Statutory TP Scheme Deduction
Raw agricultural land (*bigha* parcels in revenue villages like Navagam or Sangasar) undergoes statutory land pooling under the GTPUD Act 1976:
- Up to **50% of the original land area (OP) is deducted** for airport runways, arterial roads, and utility trenches.
- You receive back a reconstituted **Final Plot (FP)** representing approximately half the original land footprint. Always price plots on the *net final plot area*, not the unpooled agricultural survey area.

### 4. AnyRoR 7/12 & Encumbrance Verification
Verify that the parcel carries an unencumbered revenue title clear of government acquisition reservations or agricultural ceiling (*Ganot*) restrictions. Cross-check the village name and survey number on the [Dholera 7/12 AnyRoR Verification Guide](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-7-12-anyror-land-records).
