## 1. The Statutory Reality: Reconstituted Final Plots vs Raw Land

The single most common mistake retail investors make in Dholera is treating plot purchases like standard suburban layouts.

Inside the 920 sq km Dholera Special Investment Region:

1. **Ordinary Panchayat NA Permissions Are Ineffective**: Outside planned areas, developers obtain Non-Agricultural (NA) permissions from the District Collector or local Gram Panchayat. **Inside Dholera SIR, the Dholera Special Investment Region Development Authority (DSIRDA) is the sole statutory planning authority under the Gujarat SIR Act, 2009.** Any private layout created without DSIRDA development permission is legally invalid.
2. **The GTPUD Act 1976 Land Pooling System**: Dholera does not acquire land through cash displacement. It pools land into Town Planning (TP) schemes. An Original Plot (OP) under a revenue survey number undergoes an average **50% statutory deduction** for arterial roads, green belts, utility corridors, and social amenities.
3. **Only Final Plots (FP) Can Be Legally Built Upon**: A buyer must receive title to a demarcated, reconstituted **Final Plot (FP)** with a defined boundary and abutting road width under a sanctioned Town Planning scheme, not an undivided share in an unpooled revenue survey parcel.

## 2. How to Verify Any Dholera Residential Project on Gujarat RERA

Under Section 3 of the Real Estate (Regulation and Development) Act, 2016 (RERA), no promoter or developer can advertise, market, book, sell, or offer for sale any plot or apartment in a real estate project exceeding 500 sq metres or 8 plots without prior registration with the **Gujarat Real Estate Regulatory Authority (GujRERA)**.

### Step-by-Step Portal Verification

Before paying a single rupee as a booking token or advance, perform this 4-step audit:

```
[Visit GujRERA Official Portal: gujrera.gujarat.gov.in]
                           │
                           ▼
             [Select "Search Projects"]
                           │
                           ▼
    [Enter Promoter Name OR RERA Registration Number]
                           │
                           ▼
  [Verify: Sanctioned Layout Plan & Escrow Bank Account]
```

1. **Visit the Official GujRERA Portal**: Navigate to [gujrera.gujarat.gov.in](https://gujrera.gujarat.gov.in). Do not rely on screenshots, brochures, or promotional PDFs provided by sales agents.
2. **Differentiate Project RERA from Agent RERA**:
   - A **Project Registration Number** is formatted as: `PR/GJ/AHMEDABAD/DHOLERA/...`
   - An **Agent / Broker License** is formatted as: `AG/GJ/...`
   - Unscrupulous brokers frequently display their *Agent RERA certificate* to give buyers the false impression that the *unapproved plotted layout* is RERA-registered. Demand the dedicated **Project Registration Certificate**.
3. **Download the Approved Layout Plan**: On the project profile page, download the statutory layout plan approved by DSIRDA and uploaded to RERA. Verify that the plot number you are buying physically exists on the sanctioned plan with its precise dimensions.
4. **Inspect the Separate 70% Escrow Account**: Verify the name of the scheduled bank and dedicated RERA account number. All buyer remittances must legally be deposited into this escrow account, not into personal accounts or third-party entity names.

## 4. The 5 Major Red Flags in Dholera Residential Pitches

If an agent or promoter makes any of the following five representations, pause your purchase immediately:

### Red Flag 1: "Pre-Launch Bookings at Discounted Rates"
Advertising, marketing, or collecting funds for a plotting scheme prior to receiving the GujRERA Project Registration number is a cognizable violation of Section 3 of RERA, carrying statutory penalties of up to 10% of the estimated project cost and potential imprisonment. "Pre-launch" offers usually mean the project lacks statutory DSIRDA development approval.

### Red Flag 2: "Agricultural Land with Future NA Promise"
Sellers may offer raw agricultural land (*kheti zameen*) on an agreement to sell (*Banakhat*) or power of attorney, promising: *"We will get the NA and RERA approval done next year."* In Dholera SIR, individual agricultural land cannot simply be "converted to NA" outside the statutory Town Planning reconstitution mechanism. You risk holding title to raw agricultural land subject to a 50% TP deduction without the right to construct.

### Red Flag 3: "Assured Rental Returns & Buyback Schemes"
Any real estate promoter offering guaranteed 12% annual rental yields or guaranteed buybacks at double the price after 3 years is operating in violation of SEBI Collective Investment Scheme (CIS) regulations and the Banning of Unregulated Deposit Schemes (BUDS) Act, 2019. Real estate returns in greenfield industrial regions depend on statutory industrial occupancy, not arbitrary promoter promises.

### Red Flag 4: "Plots in TP 3, 4, 5 Sold as Ready-to-Build"
While TP 1 and parts of TP 2 have live trunk utilities (underground electrical trenches, water pipelines, automated sensor nodes), TP 3 through TP 6 are in earlier planning and execution phases. Buying a plot in TP 5 expecting to construct an immediate villa or residence is unrealistic until secondary trunk infrastructure packages are awarded and completed.

### Red Flag 5: "Exclusive Private Gate onto National Expressway 8"
Promoters whose boundary touches NE 8 often market "Direct Expressway Access." The National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) enforces strict access control. Private gate cuts onto the expressway carriageway are illegal. Access must route through gazetted Town Planning internal roads.

## 5. DGDCR 2024 Residential Building Regulations: FAR & Height

When purchasing a residential plot in Dholera SIR, your building envelope is governed by the **Dholera General Development Control Regulations (DGDCR 2024)**:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│           Abutting TP Road Width (Metres)              │
├─────────────────┬──────────────────┬───────────────────┤
│    12m – 18m    │    30m Road      │    55m+ Road      │
│  Base FAR: 1.5  │  Base FAR: 1.8   │  Base FAR: 2.2    │
│ Max Height: 15m │ Max Height: 25m  │ Max Height: 45m+  │
└─────────────────┴──────────────────┴───────────────────┘
```

1. **Permissible FAR (Floor Area Ratio)**: Base FAR for residential plots ranges from **1.5 to 2.2**, depending on whether the plot abuts an 18m, 30m, or 55m Town Planning road. Higher chargeable FAR (up to 3.0 or 4.0 in High Access Corridors) can be purchased from DSIRDA upon payment of statutory betterment and premium charges.
2. **Ground Coverage Limits**: Maximum ground coverage in residential zones typically caps at **40% to 50%**, ensuring mandatory setbacks, internal parking, and green coverage.
3. **Underground Infrastructure Hookups**: Every plot in an approved TP scheme must connect to the centralized SCADA-managed utility trenches (potable water, recycled water, underground power, high-speed fiber, and vacuum sewerage).

## 6. The Buyer's 7-Point Due Diligence Checklist

Before executing any sale deed or paying an advance:

- [ ] **GujRERA Registration Verified**: Confirmed project registration certificate on [gujrera.gujarat.gov.in](https://gujrera.gujarat.gov.in).
- [ ] **DSIRDA Development Permission**: Verified that the layout has written approval from DSIRDA under DGDCR 2024.
- [ ] **Final Plot (FP) Allotment Order**: Confirmed the statutory FP number and Village Form 7/12 record indicating non-agricultural status under the GTPUD Act.
- [ ] **Boundary Demarcation on Ground**: Verified physical survey pegs or digital coordinate reticles against the [DholeraMap Interactive GIS Viewer](https://dholeramap.com/map).
- [ ] **Abutting Road Width Confirmation**: Confirmed that the access road width (18m, 30m, etc.) is gazetted and unencumbered.
- [ ] **Title Search Report (30-Year Encumbrance Certificate)**: Issued by a registered advocate practicing in Dhandhuka / Ahmedabad Revenue Court confirming nil mortgages or litigation.
- [ ] **RERA Escrow Account Payment**: Ensured all booking funds are payable exclusively to the official RERA-designated escrow bank account.
