The single biggest connectivity multiplier for Dholera SIR is no longer a render on a
brochure. The **Ahmedabad–Dholera Expressway (NE 8) is operational**, inaugurated by Prime
Minister Narendra Modi on **31 March 2026**.

## What it is

| Parameter | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Designation | **NE 8** (National Expressway 8) |
| Length | **109 km** |
| Carriageway | 4-lane, **expandable to eight** |
| Access | Access-controlled |
| Maintained by | National Highways Authority of India (NHAI) |
| Status | **Operational since 31 March 2026** |
| Construction cost | ~₹3,196 crore (excluding land) |
| Corridor | Part of the **Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC)** |

The road runs from **Sardar Patel Ring Road near Sarkhej in Ahmedabad** south to **Adhelai
village in Bhavnagar district**, passing through Dholera SIR. It opened for public testing
in **February 2026** before the formal inauguration.

> **Not to be confused with NH-751.** Dholera SIR also has a separately notified 159 km
> national highway, **NH-751**, gazetted in 2019 as a secondary route of NH-51. It runs
> broadly parallel through the same corridor (Bhavnagar – Bavaliyari – Dholera – Ambali –
> Vataman – Sarkhej). The 109 km access-controlled expressway described here is **NE 8**,
> and the two are frequently conflated in property brochures. Check which one a seller
> means before paying an "expressway frontage" premium.

## The practical impact

> Travel time between Ahmedabad and Dholera has fallen from roughly **2 hours to about
> 45 minutes**.

For land valuation that is not a convenience — it is a structural change. A site that was a
two-hour haul from Gujarat's commercial capital is now a commuting distance, which is
precisely the condition under which serviced industrial and residential parcels re-price.

## How it was built

NHAI split the work into four EPC packages, awarded in **August 2020**:

| Package | Section | Length | Contractor |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Sarkhej – Sindhrej | 22.0 km | Sadbhav Engineering |
| 2 | Sindhrej – Vejalaka | 26.5 km | Sadbhav Engineering |
| 3 | Vejalaka – Dholera SIR | 22.5 km | GHV India |
| 4 | Dholera SIR – Adhelai | 38.0 km | DRA Infracon |

Tenders were floated in April 2020 and work was reported in progress by April 2022 — a
remarkably fast greenfield delivery by Indian standards, and one reason land prices in the
corridor have firmed up as fast as they have.

## The airport it serves

The **Dholera International Airport** lies on the same corridor near **Navagam**, about
**20 km from Dholera SIR** and 80 km from Ahmedabad.

| Parameter | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Site | **1,426 hectares** near Navagam, Dholera taluka |
| Operator | Dholera International Airport Company Ltd (DIACL) |
| Ownership | AAI 51%, Gujarat 33%, DMICDC 16% |
| Runway | **3,200 m**, code **4E** |
| Phase 1 cost | ₹1,305 crore (Cabinet, June 2022) |
| Terminal EPC | ₹333 crore to Yashnand Engineers (January 2024) |
| Commissioning | Targeted **December 2026** |

Phase 1 civil works — the runway, taxiways, and apron — went to **Varaha Infrastructure**
in June 2021 at ₹636.45 crore. In January 2024, DIACL awarded a **₹333 crore EPC contract
to Yashnand Engineers** for the integrated passenger terminal, ATC tower, and cargo
complex, with an 18-month construction deadline.

**75 hectares of government land is allocated for commercial development** at the airport,
which is the development most directly comparable to an aerotropolis play — and the reason
TP 5 (Sangasar, Umargadh, Zankhi) is zoned for aviation support, MRO, and air cargo.

The airport's stated purpose is to serve DSIR logistics and to relieve congestion at
Ahmedabad's Sardar Vallabhbhai Patel International Airport.

## Access-controlled does not mean you have access

This is the most misunderstood feature of the expressway, and it costs buyers real money.

**"Access-controlled" means the highway is designed to exclude at-grade crossings and random
entry.** Traffic enters and exits only at designated **interchanges**. So a plot that *touches*
the expressway boundary may have **no legal direct access to it at all** — you cannot simply
cut a gate into the verge.

Before you pay a premium for "expressway frontage", establish:

1. **Where is the nearest interchange**, and how far is the plot from it by public road?
2. **Does the plot abut a service road or a village road** that connects to that interchange?
   A plot hemmed in between the expressway and a railway or canal can be effectively
   landlocked despite a marvellous map position.
3. **What does the TP scheme show as the abutting road?** Under DGDCR your permissible
   **FAR (1.20–4.00)** and building height (**15–70 m**) follow the *abutting road width*, not
   the proximity to a highway you cannot enter. Verify it on the
   [TP map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-tp-map).

> **The premium follows usable access, not a boundary line.** A plot one kilometre from an
> interchange on a 30 m TP road is usually worth more than a plot physically touching the
> expressway with no way in.

## Which villages the corridor lifts

The expressway corridor runs through TP 2, which is why the **Pipli interchange hub** and the
**Hebatpur semiconductor fab corridor** villages command the strongest premiums. On the
village side of the equation:

- **Pipli** and **Otariya** (high-access / interchange positions) sit at the top of the
  TP 2/3 asking band.
- **Hebatpur** and **Bhimnath** carry fab-corridor and rail-terminal premiums.
- **TP 5 aerotropolis villages** — Sangasar, Umargadh, Zankhi — are the airport's direct
  catchment, but their pricing reflects an airport that is still under commissioning.

## The wider corridor: RRTS and DMIC

The expressway is one of three corridor investments, not the only one:

- **Delhi–Mumbai Industrial Corridor (DMIC)** — Dholera SIR is a designated node, which is
  the statutory frame within which the trunk infrastructure is funded.
- **Regional Rapid Transit System (RRTS)** — a planned dedicated passenger and freight
  corridor connecting the region to Ahmedabad, intended to move industrial freight off the
  highway network.
- **The expressway (NE 8)** — the piece that is *already operational*, and therefore the
  only one currently priced into land.

The practical implication: the expressway premium is real and delivered, while RRTS remains
planned. **Pay for what is built, and treat the rest as upside**, not as a justification for
today's price.
