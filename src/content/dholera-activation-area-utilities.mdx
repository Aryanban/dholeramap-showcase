Most of Dholera SIR is still statutory intent — sanctioned on paper, built out in phases.
The **Activation Area is the exception**, and it is the single most important fact for
anyone pricing a plot here.

## What the Activation Area is

A **22.5 sq km** zone spanning **TP 1 and TP 2A**, designated as the immediately
developable core of the city. It is administered under the Gujarat Special Investment
Region Act 2009, with the development executed by **DICDL** (the SPV owned 51% by Gujarat
through DSIRDA and 49% by the Centre through the DMIC Trust, established 28 January 2016).

This is where the region's "plug-and-play" claim is actually true: trunk infrastructure is
**completed and operational**, not proposed.

## What is actually in the ground

| Utility | Status in the Activation Area |
| --- | --- |
| Underground power | **Complete** |
| Dual-supply potable + recycled water | **Complete** |
| Storm-water drainage | **Complete** |
| Effluent collection network | **Complete** |
| Optical fibre / ICT | **Complete** |
| SCADA monitoring | **Complete** |
| All-weather internal roads | **Complete** |

The practical translation: a plot inside this zone can be connected to serviced utilities
without waiting for the authority to build them. That is the entire reason serviced
industrial parcels in the Activation Area trade at **₹21,000/sq yd** while equivalent
unserviced industrial land elsewhere in the SIR trades at ₹6,000 — a 3.5× spread driven by
infrastructure, not by the zone label.

## Why "plug-and-play" is a legal fact, not marketing

For an industrial allottee, the difference is direct:

- **Non-agricultural (NA) permission** on a serviced final plot is a procedural matter,
  because the land is pre-planned under a sanctioned TP scheme with completed trunk
  infrastructure.
- Building permissions follow **DGDCR** rules tied to the abutting road width — FAR from
  **1.20 to 4.00**, height from **15 m to 70+ m**. The full matrix is in the
  [TP schemes guide](https://dholeramap.com/guide).
- There is no waiting on an external utility agency to energise the site.

## The build-out gradient across the six schemes

Not all of the SIR is the Activation Area. The honest gradient, as of 2026:

| Scheme | Villages | Build-out |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **TP 1** | Ambli, Kadipur, Bhadiyad, Gogla | **Most built out** — Activation Area core |
| **TP 2A / 2B** | Hebatpur, Bhadiyad, Bavaliyari, Bhimnath | **Most built out** — fab and rail corridor |
| **TP 4A** | Bhangadh, Bhimtalav, Mundi | Advanced — solar and logistics |
| **TP 4 / TP 5** | Sangasar, Umargadh, Zankhi | Gradual |
| **TP 3** | Dholera town, Sandhida | Gradual — commercial core |
| **TP 6** | Khun, southern Bavaliyari | Early — coastal industrial |

All six schemes are at **draft TP stage**; final TP work is still in progress. That
distinction matters legally: until a scheme is final, boundaries and final plot numbers can
still adjust during reconstitution.

## The renewable backbone

Dholera's power story is not just grid supply. The region hosts an **ultra-mega solar park
of 4,400 MW** planned capacity, of which **Phase 1 (1,000 MW) is under implementation** and
**300 MW has been commissioned by Tata Power**. This is why TP 4 is zoned for renewable
energy, solar-park logistics, and green hydrogen.

Separately, the **Gujarat Data Centre Policy 2026–29**, notified in July 2026, names
Dholera its **primary hub**, with officials citing potential capacity of **7–8 gigawatts**
and investment of **₹6–7 lakh crore**. Data centres are the natural offtaker of the
reliable power and fibre the Activation Area already has.

## How to tell if a plot is actually serviced

A broker will say "plug-and-play" about anything. Verify it yourself:

1. **Locate the parcel** on the [DholeraMap cadastral atlas](https://dholeramap.com) and
   identify its TP scheme and sub-scheme.
2. **Check the build-out gradient** above — TP 1 / TP 2A / TP 4A are the zones where the
   "serviced" claim holds.
3. **Read the abutting road width** on the [TP map](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-tp-map) —
   this sets your DGDCR building envelope, so it is the other half of the value question.
4. **Confirm the Form 4/5 final plot schedule** against the
   [7/12 record](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-7-12-anyror-land-records) — a "serviced"
   plot that has not completed reconstitution is still a moving target.
5. **Price the final plot**, accounting for the ~50% OP-to-FP deduction. The full method is
   in the [September 2026 plot price guide](https://dholeramap.com/dholera-plot-price).
